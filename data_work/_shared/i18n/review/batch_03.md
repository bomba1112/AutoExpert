# Review of llm/batch_03.json (NHTSA recall summaries, RU/AZ)

Items checked: 78 (all `recall_summary`). Items changed: 20. Field edits: 30 in 29 fields (RU 14, AZ 16; `9af1aadf6e8e` az has two edits).

Check after the edits: `scripts/i18n_batches.py check 03` -> 78 pass, 0 fail. The same 15 GLOSSARY lines as before the review remain, and all are false positives of the stem check. They come from inflected forms ("ремней безопасности", "шин"), from a term inside a longer term ("seat" in "seat belt", "engine" in "engine compartment" = моторный отсек, "steering" in "steering knuckle" = поворотный кулак, "wheel" in "steering wheel" = sükan çarxı and in "wheel rim" = колёсный диск, "frame" in "sub-frame" = подрамник, "heater" in "seat heater" = нагревательный элемент сиденья), or from a model name ("Optima Plug-in Hybrid").

A script compared the model years, the production dates (day, month, year and from/to order per model in all 8 dated items), the campaign numbers (20V-519, 18V-601, 23V-480), the FMVSS numbers and the model names in all 78 items. No number is missing, changed or added, and no model name was dropped. Two source typos were corrected in both languages and were kept: "Toytoa" -> Toyota (`6f04f9eb7dc1`) and "EClass" -> E-Class (`138f93739ca5`). The Azerbaijani ordinal suffixes after every year (-ci/-cı/-cü) were checked and are correct. The FMVSS titles match batches 01-02 (108, 111, 126, 135, 138, 208, 401). Titles new in this batch (104, 201, 206, 209, 210, and 110 in its "for Passenger Cars" wording) were checked against the English.

## Most serious errors

1. `9af1aadf6e8e` ru + az (Nissan VC-Turbo engine bearings): the RU attached "с турбонаддувом и переменной степенью сжатия (VC-Turbo)" only to the 2.0 engine, but the source applies it to both the 1.5 and the 2.0 engine. The AZ "Mühərrikin yastıqlarında" is the usual Azerbaijani name of the engine mounts, not the bearings inside the engine. The units were also left as "1.5L" and "2.0L".
2. `534fbfd3a2f5`, `8a789f1f35da` ru + az (Hyundai/Kia accessory trailer hitch harness): "жгутом ..., в котором ... стоп-сигналы прицепа могут гореть" / "ki, ... onda qoşqunun stop-siqnalları" placed the trailer brake lights inside the harness. The AZ also dropped the genitive ("yedəkləmə qurğusu naqil dəstəsi").
3. `e40f5a04843e` az (Range Rover PHEV coolant elbow pipe): "mühərrik mühərrikin soyuducu maye dirsək borusunun" is a broken noun chain with a repeated subject. It is now "mühərrikdə soyuducu mayenin dirsəkli borusu partlaya bilər".
4. `06d936839a6f` ru (Kia limp mode): "режим движения автомобиля с ограниченной подвижностью" read as "the drive mode of a vehicle with limited mobility". The source means a limited-mobility (limp) mode. `518147615993` az: "amortizatorların boşalması" reads as the shock absorbers going flat, while the source says their mounting loosens.
5. Russian units and calques: `c8d4acee91f6`, `53fae87de639` left "2.5L" in Russian (now "2,5 л", AZ "2,5 l"). In `73797e48f869` and `35807ff3c068`, "подвергнуть электрический разъём коррозии" is a calque of "expose to corrosion". In `4f17042313a4`, "соединение может разъединиться" is the same tautology fixed in batch 02. In `88ac87656adf` az, "yanılı" is not a standard Azerbaijani word.

## Noted, not changed

- `adf15e706e10` ru: the company name is transliterated («Тойота Мотор Инжиниринг энд Мэньюфэкчуринг Норт Америка, Инк.»), while every other item keeps the legal name in Latin. The source writes "manufacturing" in lower case. The check rejects a lower-case Latin word in RU ("untranslated") and a capitalised "Manufacturing" that is not in this source text, so the Latin name cannot pass here. Fixing this needs the check in `scripts/i18n_build.py` to accept lower-case words inside a company name.
- `1252e8fea027` ru: "A4 Олроуд". This is the same check limit as `ecab56d83309` in batch 02, because the source writes "allroad" in lower case. The AZ keeps "A4 allroad".
- AZ dates in `534fbfd3a2f5`, `8bf88137ae83`, `a20ddd1cbeae` and `35ec583b2e35` are numeric ("19.05.2015-ci il tarixindən"). The other dated items and batch 02 write "day month year". A test conversion failed the check in all four items: the Azerbaijani month "may" collides with the English function word "may" in `EN_FUNCTION`, so the numeric format was restored. The numeric form is correct official Azerbaijani, and all dates were verified.
- `6f04f9eb7dc1` ru/az keep the Toyota part name "Skid Control ECU", next to a translated "блок управления" / "idarəetmə bloku". This is the manufacturer's component name, and the check accepts it.
- `7234dd8fecff` "потере управляемости" / "idarəetmənin itirilməsi" for "loss of steering control" is standard recall wording and was kept.
