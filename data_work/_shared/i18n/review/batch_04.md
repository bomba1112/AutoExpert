# Review of llm/batch_04.json (NHTSA recall summaries, RU/AZ)

Items checked: 78 (all `recall_summary`). Items changed: 18. Field edits: 22 in 19 fields (RU 7 edits in 6 fields, AZ 15 edits in 13 fields; `13f5ac48faf9` ru, `1f61af1ebd64` az and `73b8f4b55a73` az have two edits each).

A backup of the file before the review was taken, and the edits were first applied to a copy. The copy passed the check, and then the same script wrote the real file. The result is identical to the copy, and the 78 kind/hash pairs and their order are unchanged.

Check after the edits: `scripts/i18n_batches.py check 04` -> 78 pass, 0 fail. The same 11 GLOSSARY lines as before the review remain, and all are false positives of the stem check. They come from a term inside a longer term: "engine" in "engine compartment" = моторный отсек; "frame" in "sub-frame" = подрамник and in "seat frame" = каркас сиденья / oturacağın karkası; "seat" in "seat belt"; "wheel" in "steering wheel" = sükan çarxı. In `c6d3755a6702`, "passenger side air bag" is the passenger-side air bag in the instrument panel, not a side air bag.

A script compared the following in all 78 items: model years; the production dates in all 12 dated items (day, month, year, and their order in RU and AZ); the campaign numbers 13V-454, 15V-246, 16V-643 and 17V-210; the FMVSS numbers 108, 110, 111, 202, 208, 225 and 401; and the model names. No number is missing, changed or added, and no model name was dropped. Where a year or model name appears fewer times in the translation, it is because RU/AZ name it once for several models. The source typo "September3" (`3e9adfce72ac`) is correctly read as 3 September. The Azerbaijani ordinal suffixes after every year (-ci/-cı/-cu/-cü) are correct. The FMVSS titles were compared with batches 01-03. The AZ titles of 110 and 401 differed and were aligned. The RU titles all match, and 202 and 225, which are new in this batch, were checked against the English.

## Most serious errors

1. `236a985da7a7` ru (Audi side marker lights): "седаны A4 и Q7 2017 модельного года" turned the Q7 crossover into a sedan. "седаны A3 и A3 е-трон" did the same to the A3 e-tron. The source names a body style only for the TT, A3 and A4. The list now reads "TT в кузове родстер, A3 в кузовах кабриолет и седан и A3 е-трон ..., A4 в кузове седан и Q7 ...".
2. `73f59471093a` ru + az (VW fuel rail): the production date range for the Passat was attached to the 1.8T engines ("с двигателями 1.8T, изготовленными с 13 ноября ..." / "... istehsal edilmiş 1.8T mühərrikləri"). In the source list, every date range is a vehicle build period (Jetta, Passat, Golf). The engine reading would send an owner to look for an engine build date that the owner cannot see.
3. `c998b01be17a` az (Ford shifter cable bushing): "sürətlər qutusunun keçid trosu" ("transition cable") does not name the part being recalled. It is now "sürətlərin dəyişdirilməsi trosu" (the gear-shift cable). `73b8f4b55a73` az (BMW ISOFIX lower anchor bars): "aşağı lövbər bərkidiciləri" used "lövbər", which means a ship's anchor. It is now "aşağı bərkidici çubuqlar", consistent with "aşağı bərkidici" in `951ee665e736`.
4. Azerbaijani words with the wrong meaning: `c4cf7c5f794b` "yanılı" is not a standard word; the same defect was fixed in batch 03. In `e25f4ba5f56e`, "düzləndirilməmiş" means "not flattened" where the source says "not aligned". In `ab744308921f`, "yayılmaya" means "not spread" where the source says the air bag does not unfold. In `e55a5952da22`, "birləşməsi ... qoşulmamış" is a tautology ("the connection was not connected"). In `1f61af1ebd64`, "şinlərin vurulma təzyiqi" uses colloquial "pumping" for the cold tire inflation pressure.
5. Consistency and Russian style: the AZ titles of FMVSS 110 ("seçimi") and FMVSS 401 ("açılma qurğusu") differed from batches 01-03 ("seçilməsi", "açılması qurğusu"). `dc2ffa123eb8` ru had the tautology "остаточными ... оставшимися". `13f5ac48faf9` ru had the word-for-word "при столкновении с ударом сзади" and swapped the model order. `df93d985ea00` ru said the plate itself "может ослабнуть". `64238ba3c38f` ru gave "excessive" humidity as "повышенной" (raised). `148e506700ab` az used the calque "mümkün olaraq qalır".

## Noted, not changed

- `236a985da7a7` ru: "A3 е-трон" is in Cyrillic, while the AZ keeps "A3 e-tron". This is the same check limit as "A4 Олроуд" in batch 03: `scripts/i18n_build.py` rejects a lower-case Latin word in RU ("untranslated").
- `d9cd0dd4fb51` az: "Sports Activity avtomobili" is half translated, while the RU keeps BMW's designation "Sports Activity Vehicle". "vehicle" is in `EN_FUNCTION`, so the check rejects the Latin designation in AZ.
- `bb0e3649ed12`: "X7 Drive50i" is very likely a source typo for "xDrive50i". It is kept as printed in both languages because it is a model designation.
- RU "колес"/"колесах" without ё in `68c3a1808590`, `7d0d961390a9`, `3d3d86ff0ee7`, `7f99f86208a6`, `56d758818075` and `be911a8afbe0`. The glossary and the built translations write "колёс". A test change on the copy made the glossary stem check ('колесо' -> 'коле') report 'wheel' as unused in 4 items, so it was not applied. ё is optional in Russian, and the text is correct either way.
- `fd4d884aa03f` and `1f61af1ebd64` ru put the model list in parentheses, while the other items use a colon or no punctuation. Without the bracket, the sentence ends in "Supra." / "M50i.", and the check reads that as a new Latin token ('Supra.', 'i.'). The parentheses were kept.
- `72d45a833e59` "стать причиной возгорания" / "yanğına səbəb ola bilər" for "may ... catch fire": the meaning is close. A verb-only version ("загореться" / "alışa bilər") drops the glossary term fire = возгорание / yanğın, so it was kept.
- `c634a7a9969d` ru: "скользящее шлицевое соединение" adds "шлицевое" (splined). A driveshaft slip joint is a splined joint, so this is the standard Russian term and was kept.
- The AZ dates in `2baa9ec8ecf9` and `c634a7a9969d` are numeric ("23.02.2016-cı il"), as in batch 03, because of the "may" collision with `EN_FUNCTION`. Both were verified.
- `c6d3755a6702` "passenger side air bag" was translated as the passenger-side air bag ("подушка безопасности со стороны пассажира"). The air bag is in the instrument panel, so this is correct, and the GLOSSARY line is a false positive.
