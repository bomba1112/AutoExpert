# Review of glossary delta 2026-10-03.3 (27 terms)

Scope: the last `changes` entry of `data_work/_shared/i18n/glossary.json` ("27 terms added for the Cadillac, Jeep and Mitsubishi texts"). That is 22 `nhtsa_component_segment` terms, "Not Shifting Properly" in `carcomplaints_problem` / `issue_topic` / `issue_topic_part`, and 4 `maintenance_qualifier` terms. `glossary.json` was not edited.

**Method**
- Each segment was judged inside the full NHTSA paths it occurs in. The paths come from `sources.json` and the composed results from `translations.json`, for example `POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:PARK/NEUTRAL START INTERLOCK SWITCH`.
- Each term was compared with the conventions and the `general` terms of the glossary, with the existing segments of the same family, and with how batches 01-15 render the same parts.

**Result:** 27 terms checked. 17 are OK and 10 should change. The two important changes are PARK/NEUTRAL START INTERLOCK SWITCH, whose meaning reads inverted, and "Not Shifting Properly", which breaks the case convention in `issue_topic` / `issue_topic_part`. The other 8 fix grammar (AZ 'təkərə ötürücü', 'səviyyəsi xəbərdarlığı'), a tautology (STEERING CONTROL MODULE), a calque ('lövbər'), or consistency with the general terms.

## Reasons for the changes

**15. PARK/NEUTRAL START INTERLOCK SWITCH (meaning).** This is the switch that lets the engine start only in P or N (the neutral safety switch). "Выключатель блокировки пуска в положениях «Парковка» и «Нейтраль»" reads as 'the switch that blocks starting in Park and Neutral', which is the opposite. The AZ "... vəziyyətlərində işəsalmanın bloklanması açarı" has the same problem.
- The quoted position names do not follow the glossary: park position = 'положение парковки (P)' / 'park mövqeyi (P)'.
- 'Parklama' is the act of parking, not the selector position. Batches 01-09 always use 'положение парковки' / 'park mövqeyi'.
- The proposal names what the switch does: it permits starting in those two positions.
- The letters P and N are not used, because a Latin word that is not in the source fails `i18n_build.check`.

**23a. Not Shifting Properly (issue_topic / issue_topic_part, case).** The conventions say that Title Case CarComplaints texts become sentence case. Every other CarComplaints entry in these two sections starts with a capital letter, for example 'Кондиционер работает неправильно' and 'Mühərrik nasazlığı lampası yanır'. This one starts lower case. In `translations.json` the `issue_symptom` "Not Shifting Properly" therefore comes out as "коробка переключает передачи неправильно". The other CarComplaints symptoms, such as "A/C Not Working", come out capitalised, and `carcomplaints_problem` already has the capital letter.

**23 / 23a. RU word order.** "Коробка переключает передачи неправильно" puts the adverb last, which stresses it. The neutral order is "Коробка неправильно переключает передачи". The AZ "ötürmələri düzgün dəyişmir" is natural ("qutu sürətləri düzgün dəyişmir"), so it stays.

**12. STEERING CONTROL MODULE.** "Блок управления рулевым управлением" / "Sükan idarəsinin idarəetmə bloku" repeat the same root ('control unit of the control'). "Электронный блок рулевого управления" / "Sükan idarəsinin elektron bloku" keep the glossary 'steering' = рулевое управление / sükan idarəsi and the generic sense of NHTSA's segment. On GM vehicles it is the power steering control module; a narrower 'рулевой механизм' could be wrong for column-mounted units.

**10. TRANSFER CASE (4-WHEEL DRIVE), AZ.** "4 təkərə ötürücü" puts the dative 'təkərə' in front of the noun 'ötürücü', which is not a well-formed phrase. "4 təkər ötürücüsü" is a normal izafet; "4" must stay for the number check. The RU "привод на 4 колеса" is correct. The same dative appeared in batch 15 `9f77f459cc07` ("dörd təkərə ötürücülü") and was corrected there.

**5. VEHICLE TETHER ANCHOR.** 'lövbər' is a ship's or construction anchor; "lövbər bərkidicisi" is a calque of 'anchor'. The glossary renders anchorages as 'bərkidilmə nöqtəsi' / 'точка крепления':
- seat belt anchorage = 'təhlükəsizlik kəmərinin bərkidilmə nöqtəsi';
- FMVSS 210 = «Təhlükəsizlik kəmərlərinin bərkidilmə nöqtələri»;
- FMVSS 225 = «Uşaq oturacaqlarının bərkidilmə sistemləri».

The RU "анкерное крепление" is understandable and in use, so the RU change is optional and only for consistency. If the term changes, batch 14 `c072657f8168` (RU "анкерным креплением верхнего ремня", AZ "lövbər bərkidicisi") should follow; it currently repeats the glossary wording. The repeated 'детское кресло' in the path "Детское кресло: анкерное крепление ... детского кресла" is acceptable; the existing VEHICLE LATCH ANCHOR does the same.

**11 and 14. SRS MALFUNCTION WARNING LAMP/LIGHT, LAMP.** The glossary has 'warning light' = 'сигнальная лампа' / 'xəbərdarlıq lampası' and 'WARNING LIGHT/DEVICES' = 'Сигнальные лампы и устройства'. 'Контрольная лампа' / 'Nəzarət lampası' is the existing rendering of INDICATOR LIGHT.
- Under BRAKE FLUID LOW WARNING, LAMP is the warning lamp. The recall text of the same campaign (b15 `5b94ad470782`) says 'сигнальную лампу' / 'xəbərdarlıq lampasını'.
- 'LAMP' is a bare key, so whatever it maps to is used for every future path ending in LAMP. If the key must also serve lighting paths, use the neutral 'Лампа' / 'Lampa' instead; the parent segment already says "warning".
- The SRS term's AZ 'nasazlıq lampası' parallels MIL = 'mühərrik nasazlığı lampası' and is fine.

**13. BRAKE FLUID LOW WARNING, AZ.** "Əyləc mayesinin aşağı səviyyəsi xəbərdarlığı" stacks a possessed noun ('səviyyəsi') directly in front of another izafet head ('xəbərdarlığı'), which is clumsy in Azerbaijani. "... aşağı səviyyəsi barədə xəbərdarlıq" is the clean form. The RU is correct.

**2. FRONT UNDERHOOD.** The comma in the AZ "Kapotun altında, ön hissədə" splits one segment into two phrases inside a colon-joined path ("Elektrik sistemi: elektrik naqilləri: kapotun altında, ön hissədə"). The glossary has 'engine compartment' = 'моторный отсек' / 'mühərrik bölməsi', which is what 'underhood' means. The RU "Под капотом спереди" is acceptable as is; the proposal only aligns it.

**25. electronic limited slip differential.** In the Cadillac CTS schedule this is "Change rear axle fluid, if equipped with electronic limited slip differential". It is a mechanical clutch-type differential with electronic control that needs its own fluid. "электронный дифференциал" reads as an 'electronic differential', the brake-based function that the existing segment ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP renders as 'электронная блокировка дифференциала'. "... с электронным управлением" / "elektron idarəli ..." keeps the two apart.

## Notes on the terms marked OK

- **DIFFERENTIAL UNIT, RADIATOR ASSEMBLY:** these follow the assembly convention ('в сборе' / 'qovşağı'), like AXLE ASSEMBLY = Мост в сборе / Körpü qovşağı.
- **UPPER BALL JOINT:** 'Üst kürəvi oynaq' pairs with the existing LOWER BALL JOINT = 'Alt kürəvi oynaq'. The recall texts use 'yuxarı asqı qolu' for upper control arms; both are correct Azerbaijani.
- **SEAT HEATER/COOLER:** 'вентиляция' / 'ventilyasiya' is the usual name of seat cooling. 'охлаждение' / 'soyutma' would be more literal but less idiomatic. The slash renders as 'и' / 'və', like FAN/MOTOR and SPEEDOMETER/ODOMETER.
- **COOLER/RADIATOR:** under AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION this is the transmission fluid cooler. The generic 'Радиатор охлаждения' / 'Soyutma radiatoru' is correct in that path and safe if the key appears elsewhere. Free text may say 'масляный радиатор коробки передач' (b13 `845c22f0ba07`).
- **HOSES AND FITTINGS, HOSE, PIPING, AND CONNECTIONS:** these match the existing HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS = Шланги, трубки и фитинги / Şlanqlar, borular və birləşdiricilər.
- **HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:** 'Гидроусилитель' is the natural term in the path "Рулевое управление: гидроусилитель", and the AZ matches 'sükan gücləndiricisi'.
- **FLOOR MAT:** 'Döşəmə xalçası' is correct, and batch 13 uses it for the same Mopar mats. The colloquial 'ayaqaltı' is not needed.
- **TRANSFER CASE (RU), PTU:** 'Раздаточная коробка' / 'Paylayıcı qutu' is also what batches 13-14 use for the Cherokee PTU. That keeps the transfer-case family consistent.
- **four wheel disc brakes, limited slip differential, if so equipped:** these are correct and consistent with 'if equipped' = при наличии / mövcud olduqda. 'дифференциал повышенного трения' is the standard RU term. The AZ 'məhdud sürüşmə diferensialı' is a transparent calque; 'özübloklanan diferensial' is the workshop alternative.
