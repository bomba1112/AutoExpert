## Most serious errors

1. `88fe32559ec6` ru (GM engine block heater cord): "оснащённые подогревателем Calix для масляного поддона / блока цилиндров" came after the Chevrolet Sonic dates. That limited the heater condition to the Sonic, so the Encore, Verano and Cruze read as recalled whether or not they had the heater. The defect is in the block heater cord, so the condition applies to every listed model, which is how the AZ already reads. The heater now heads the list. The heading "2013–2014 модельных годов" also no longer covers the 2012–2014 Sonic.
2. `1c79655a4a81` ru (GM OCS, ventilated seats): "может ненадлежащим образом отключать подушку безопасности" said the air bag may switch off in a wrong way. The risk in the source is the opposite: the air bag may stay on with a small child or child seat in place ("may not properly disable"). Now "может не отключать должным образом".
3. `a77da39699a3` ru (Malibu HVAC, FMVSS 103): "обогрев ветрового стекла" names an electrically heated windshield, which is a different system. Now "система удаления наледи с ветрового стекла", which matches the FMVSS 103 title.
4. AZ wording that was not Azerbaijani or meant something else: `16f4b9fb0001` "yan zərbə təhlükəsizlik yastıqları" stacks three nouns with no izafet ending; the glossary has yan təhlükəsizlik yastığı. In `b7fe9dd51547`, "toz örtük" can be read as a dust cover; powder coating is "toz boya örtüyü". In `5e0499ca4798` (FMVSS 305), "elektrolitin dağılması" means scattering; spillage is "tökülməsi". In `aa79d91a9fb0`, "Servis xidməti" is a tautology. In `5ff902638dca`, "bəzi" appeared twice in one clause, and "kifayət qədər olmayan miqdarda ... agentlə" was a calque.
5. Wrong part or wrong action named in RU/AZ: `c60177e4f887` ru "стойка салазок переднего сиденья водителя в сборе" made the whole seat the assembly; the source names the seat track assembly. In `09a6ce539f42` ru + az, "раскрытие второй ступени" / "ikinci pillənin açılması": the inflator stage fires, it does not unfold. In `0860a884cce9` and `4397c54f966e` az, the Ford shifter cable was "sürət qolunun trosu", while batch 04 (c998b01be17a) fixed the same part as "sürətlərin dəyişdirilməsi trosu". In `ad97d1a18f8f` ru + az, the rear-impact calque "столкновение с ударом сзади" / "arxadan zərbəli toqquşma" is the same one batch 04 fixed.
